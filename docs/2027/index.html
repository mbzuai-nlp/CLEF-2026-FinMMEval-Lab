Tasks

Teams can enter any of the three tasks independently.

FinExam

Professional financial reasoning

Answer multiple-choice questions aligned with the CFA and FRM curricula. Questions can include case vignettes, tables, and other exhibits.

Submission
One answer option per question.
Primary metric
Answer accuracy.

FinNorm

Financial rules across frameworks

Choose the correct answer to a financial scenario under an explicitly stated Islamic or conventional framework.

Submission
One answer option per scenario.
Primary metric
Joint accuracy on framework adherence and rule application.

FinTrade

Evidence-grounded trading

Verify timestamped news, identify the affected commodity, and decide whether to trade in a simulated market with common prices and transaction fees.

Submission
Orders and evidence-backed judgments about claims, commodities, and price effects.
Primary metric
Normalized gain over holding the initial portfolio.
